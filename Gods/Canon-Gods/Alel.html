Alel

The Supreme Patriarch, Former Sovereign of Aeloria, and Bearer of the Mantle of Creation

Overview

Alel was the supreme patriarch and original ruler of the ancient divine monarchy of Aeloria, the predecessor to modern-day Kristiania. He was one of the most powerful and influential divine beings in the history of the Divine Order, governing an extensive divine hierachy through his authority over the Divine Council, which mostly consisted of his children.

Alel was the original creator and wielder of the Mantle of Creation, a metaphysical artefact that established his supreme authority over the divine monarchy and protected him against threats.

Background

As the supreme authority of the old divine monarchy, Alel ruled over hundreds of divine beings, each possessing their own territories, responsibilities, followers, and authority. Alel was the one who had decided how the territories were split in the beginning. His administration was intended to maintain order through cooperation, representation and a structured distribution of divine responsibilities.

Despite his immense power and authority, Alel's monarchy became increasingly dysfunctional, corrupted and fractured. This was due to the increasing amount of members of the Divine Council which resulted in conflicting ambitions, territorial disputes, political instability and many disagreements, eventually undermining his authority.
