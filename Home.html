A philosophical fantasy world currently governed by four divine nations, each ruled by an "almighty", "good" god who serves as its supreme authority, protector and symbol of legitimacy. Each nation has their main god and candidate goddess. In this world, unknown to the general population, humanity actually predates the gods and through faith, collective conviction, and generations of religious devotion, humans established metaphysical connections with divine beings from an independent plane of existence. This allows them to manifest in the physical world and exercise extraodinary powers. These gods are not creators of existence, but beings whose authority, influence, and power depend on the continued recognition and support of humanity. Overtime, the gods and humans have forgotten this relationship, to the point where gods are seen as more powerful beings than who they truly are.

All content is aimed to be secular, informative and objective when needed. Mostly to serve as allegories.
