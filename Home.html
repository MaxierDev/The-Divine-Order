A fantasy world currently governed by four divine nations, each ruled by an "almighty", "good" god who serves as its supreme authority, protector and symbol of legitimacy. Each nation has their main god and candidate goddess. In this world, unknown to the general population, humanity actually predates the gods and through faith, collective conviction, and generations of religious devotion, humans established metaphysical connections with divine beings from an independent plane of existence. This allows them to manifest in the physical world and exercise extraordinary powers. These gods are not creators of existence, but beings whose authority, influence, and power depend on the continued recognition and support of humanity. Over time, the gods and humans have forgotten this relationship, to the point where gods are seen as more powerful beings than who they truly are.

Disclaimer: When discussing religious, historical, and philosophical topics, the intention is to maintain a secular, informative, and historically objective perspective while using fictional personifications, allegory, and satire to critically examine their ideologies, philosophies, institutions, and historical developments.
