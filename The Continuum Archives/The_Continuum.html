Actual Password Manager

During Asgear's influence building, one of his brilliant manipulations were at an institutional level. When attacking the cybersecurity of the 4 nations, Asgear used many trojan horses. His alias was Elias Veyron, a respected academic in the cybersecurity world.

Contributions included:

  • Analysis of password security
  • Discussions of authentication standards
  • Mathematical explanations of password entropy
  • Criticisms of outdated security assumptions
  • Reviews of organisational cybersecurity policies

Elias Veyron started with smaller organisations, where they gave independent assessments, identifying ordinary problems and providing useful recommendations.

Through connections, these recommendations made it to the 4 nations and their overall cybersecurity department.

Asgear introduced cybersecurity policies which looked beneficial but in reality resulted in vulnerabilities that only Asgear was aware of. One of these trojan horses were password managers, although there are others.

While Asgear did not gain support of all the cybersecurity experts, he managed to convince the decision makers of 2 nations for a full implementation, 1 nation for a trial, and one nation had rejected the proposal.

Since Asgear gained insight into how the passwords were managed to generated, it made it easier for Asgear to infiltrate certain databases.

Therefore there are two password managers. One password manager was overly long, playing on the intuitive thinking of longer and more complicated passwords are better, and Asgear's actual password for The Continuum which is more basic but effective.
